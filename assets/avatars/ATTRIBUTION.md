# Avatar attribution

The avatar images in this directory are generated with [DiceBear](https://www.dicebear.com/)
from the **Personas** style.

- **Style:** Personas by [Draftbit](https://draftbit.com/) — <https://personas.draftbit.com/>
- **License:** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Remix:** via [DiceBear](https://www.dicebear.com/styles/personas/) (`@dicebear/styles@10.6.0`)

Each PNG was generated deterministically from a role seed (e.g. `coder`, `reviewer`),
a gender pool (female / male) and a skin tone from the Personas palette. The resolved
style options for every file are recorded in `manifest.json`.

Generation parameters: DiceBear `personas` (`@dicebear/styles@10.6.0`), 256 px,
circular pastel background (`borderRadius=50`, colors from the DiceBear pastel set),
one of 50 role seeds, a gender pool (female = long-hair variants with no facial hair;
male = short-hair variants with 65% facial hair), and a skin tone from the Personas
palette. The pool was curated by hand after generation.
